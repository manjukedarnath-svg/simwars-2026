Competition Awareness & Readiness

This part helps us understand your familiarity with the SimWars competition format, rules, and public setting. Your responses will directly inform the opening briefing and clarify confusing rules.

Confidential – organising team only 5–7 minutes
How SimWars 2026 differs from standard teaching simulation
AspectStandard Teaching SimulationSimWars 2026
Primary PurposeLearning & skill development – practice, make mistakes, improve in a safe spaceAssessment & competition – perform at your best under pressure; scores determine advancement
StakesLow – mistakes expected and welcomedHigh – mistakes are penalised; performance has tangible consequences
AudienceTypically closedPublic – live audience watches via projector; participants are "on stage"
DebriefingIn‑depth, confidential, balancedTwo‑tier: public positive‑only debrief + private written feedback from judges
Judging / ScoringFacilitators give informal feedback – no numeric scoreFormal structured scoring by expert judges using standardised rubrics
ResourcesUnlimited time, no real resource constraintsTime‑bound (15‑minute hard stop) with explicit resource decisions
Psychological DemandLow to moderate – "safe to fail"Higher – public observation, formal scoring, time pressure
Manikin UseParticipants may be guided or promptedNo prompting – you must demonstrate all actions – PLEASE DO, don't just ask
