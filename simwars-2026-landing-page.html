About the Competition

What is SIM WARS?

Sim Wars is a coliseum of the mind—where the art of simulation meets the spirit of competition. Born from a belief that learning flourishes in the crucible of challenge, it is a stage where practitioners rise, teams unite, and human factors take center stage. Across India, from Delhi to Pune, Sim Wars has become a movement—not of speed or scores, but of skill, teamwork, and the quiet courage to learn in public. It is not merely a competition; it is a celebration of clinical excellence, a sanctuary for growth, and a reminder that in the theatre of medicine, we are all students, forever learning together.

SIM WARS is PediSTARS India's flagship simulation championship, run alongside SIMULUS 11 — the 11th Annual National Conference of PediSTARS on Healthcare Simulation. Sixteen teams of pediatric residents, fellows, nurses and paramedics face off in high-fidelity emergency scenarios across three escalating rounds, judged live by an expert panel against a published rubric, to be crowned champion of Pediatric & Neonatal SimWars 2026.

What to Expect
22 Aug · DHEE Hospitals

Preliminary Round

All 16 teams run two separate 15-minute high-fidelity pediatric emergency scenarios, expected to lead with a rapid, systematic ABCDE assessment, accurate rhythm and airway recognition, and timely, correctly-sequenced pharmacological and electrical therapies. Cases evolve in real time, so teams must reassess at regular intervals and adapt their management as the clinical picture changes — all while maintaining closed-loop communication, clear role delegation, and a crisp, structured handover at the end of each case.

  • Clinical Judgement 40
  • Crisis Resource Management 50
  • Global Entrustment 10

Scored per case · two cases per team

23 Aug · VASA

Semi-Finals

The top 4 teams advancing from prelims step up to a higher-acuity, higher-complexity 15-minute scenario. The core test is prioritization — allocating attention, personnel and resources correctly as the clinical picture evolves, justifying those calls out loud, and communicating clearly with the family throughout. Two clinical and two non-clinical judges score independently; each domain is the average of both judges' marks, not the sum.

  • Clinical Judgement 40
  • Crisis Resource Management 50
  • Global Entrustment 10
23 Aug · VASA

Finals

The two finalist teams face a single championship-level scenario that pushes advanced physiological reasoning — teams are expected to think and reason out loud as a unit, defend each intervention with clear target thresholds, and escalate through the treatment ladder with sound justification. Followership is tested as rigorously as leadership: speaking up clearly and constructively when something doesn't add up is scored in its own right, alongside sustained, empathetic communication with the family throughout.

  • Clinical Management 45
  • Teamwork & Followership 40
  • Shared Reasoning 15

Team Composition & Rules

Who's Competing

  16 Teams Confirmed

Composition per Team

  • 1 team = 4 members. At least 2 doctors should be postgraduate trainees.
  • Minimum of one nurse; the remaining member can be a nurse, paramedic, postgraduate trainee, fellow/DrNB/DM, or junior consultant/assistant professor.
  • All physicians and residents must have less than 5 years of experience post-MD/DNB/equivalent degree.
  • No experience limit for nurses and paramedics.

Team Flexibility

  • Teams can include members from different hospitals of the same organization.
  • A team cannot include more than one person who competed in the immediately previous SimWars competition.
