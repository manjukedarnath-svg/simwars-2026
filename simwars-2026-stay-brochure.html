🛏️ Where to stay — hostel to luxury

🎒

Hostels · ₹600–1,500/bed

Zostel & backpacker hostels around Church Street / MG Road; The Hosteller & similar in Indiranagar. Right on the Purple Line.

🏨

Budget hotels · ₹1,500–3,500

Treebo / FabHotel / OYO Townhouse around Majestic & Gandhinagar — at the interchange itself; one train to either venue.

🏠

Airbnbs · ₹2,500–5,500

1BHKs in Indiranagar, Ulsoor / Trinity — metro at the doorstep, easy for a team of 4 to split.

✨

Premium · ₹8,000+

Taj MG Road, Vivanta / The Ritz-Carlton (Residency Rd), ITC Gardenia — a short hop from MG Road metro.

🛌

On-campus at VASA

Vydehi campus guest house / hostel — ideal for Day 2. Availability & rates: vasa.info@vimsmail.com

Book early — August is conference season. Verify prices when booking. Report to the venue 15 minutes early on both days.
