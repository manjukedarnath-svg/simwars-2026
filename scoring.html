⚖️ Combined-Score Tie-Break Policy

No oral tiebreaker question. Ties are resolved automatically, in this order:

  1. Combined Score — BLS weighted total + PALS weighted total (out of 200).
  2. If still tied — the higher individual PALS weighted total wins (PALS is the designated tie-break axis, as the CRM/non-technical-weighted case).
  3. If still tied — the higher combined Domain III (Global Entrustment, BLS + PALS, out of 20) wins.
  4. If still tied — manual Chief panel discussion of safety-flag counts.
